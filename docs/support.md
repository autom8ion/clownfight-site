# Clown Fight Support

Thanks for playing! If something's wrong or you have a question, email **avzrule@gmail.com** and include your device
(for example "iPhone 15" or "iPad Air") and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**How do I play?**
On the title screen, choose HOW TO PLAY. In a fight, pause and choose MOVE LIST to see every move. The practice round
before the first fight teaches the basics.

**How do two people play?**
Open GAME MODES and choose 2 PLAYERS. On iPad, one player can use the touch controls while the other uses a game
controller, or both can use controllers. On iPhone, connect two game controllers. 2 PLAYERS only appears
once two players can actually play.

**Which controllers work?**
Any controller that works with iPhone or iPad, such as Xbox and PlayStation controllers or MFi controllers.
Pair it in your device's Bluetooth settings.

**Several kids share one device. Can each have their own game?**
Yes. Up to six players. On the title screen, choose PLAYER to add, switch or remove a kid. Each has their own look,
progress and settings.

**Is there anything to buy, or any ads?**
No. You pay once, and that's everything.

**Do I need Game Center or an internet connection?**
No. The whole game works offline and without Game Center. Signed in to Game Center, you also get achievements and
leaderboards.

**The flashes bother me or my child.**
Open OPTIONS and turn on CALMER FLASHES. If anyone feels dizzy or unwell while playing, stop and rest. A very small
number of people can have seizures from flashing lights, even if it has never happened before. If that's a worry,
check with a doctor first.

**The sound or music is too loud, or I want it off.**
OPTIONS has MUSIC and SOUND switches. The game also follows your device's silent switch and volume.

**I play left-handed.**
Turn on LEFT-HANDED in OPTIONS to swap the stick and the buttons.

**Did I lose my progress?**
Progress is saved after every fight, on the device. It comes back when you restore the device from a backup. It's
deleted if you delete the app.

**Privacy**
The game collects no data. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
